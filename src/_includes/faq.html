FAQs

Common questions about our tutoring programs and sessions

What subjects do you offer?
We offer tutoring for all subjects from Prep to Year 10. For Years 11–12, we offer Mathematics and Physics. We also provide preparation for NAPLAN, Selective Entry and Scholarship Exams.
How many students are in a group?
We keep our classes small, with a maximum of 8 students. This allows each student to receive individual attention and support.
Can tutoring help my child if they are already doing well?
Yes! Tutoring can help students improve their skills, build confidence and prepare for exams.
What if my child is struggling?
We identify areas where your child needs help and provide the right support to improve their skills and confidence.
How are sessions scheduled?
Sessions are held after school on weekdays. We offer flexible times to suit your family’s schedule.
How do you measure my child’s progress?
We monitor progress through regular assessments, homework and feedback. Progress reports are also available to keep parents informed.
How do I sign up?
Simply contact us by phone or through our website. We’ll discuss your child’s needs and help you choose the right program.
